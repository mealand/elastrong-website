Rice, beans, oil & more
01

Food & nutrition

Hunger is the most urgent need we meet. Each outreach delivers food packs and shares hot meals together.

  • Monthly food packs
  • Shared hot meals on outreach days
  • Nutritional supplements for the frail
770+ people treated
02

Medical outreach

Our 13+ medical volunteers bring free healthcare to everyone in underserved communities. Most recently: Kafanchan Emirate Ward A & B, Jema’a LGA, Kaduna State.

  • Free malaria testing & treatment
  • Free dental check-ups
  • Free medical consultations
  • Blood pressure check-ups
  • Blood sugar level check-ups
Comfort & safety
03

Clothing & essentials

Small things make a big difference: warm clothes, a mattress off the floor, a net against malaria.

  • Clothes, shoes & wrappers
  • Toiletries & hygiene kits
  • Mattresses, blankets & mosquito nets
Time & presence
04

Companionship

Loneliness wounds as deeply as hunger. We listen, laugh, pray and celebrate with the senior citizens we serve.

  • Home visits & check-in calls
  • Birthday & festive celebrations
  • Sponsor-a-senior-citizen relationships
Project Clean
05

Hygiene & healthy living

Through Project Clean, volunteers roll up their sleeves to sweep, scrub and wash, leaving senior citizens’ homes and surroundings clean, safe and healthy.

  • Cleaning homes & compounds
  • Washing clothes & bedding
  • Hygiene kits & healthy-living tips

How an outreach works

From discovery to follow-up.

1

Identify

Community leaders and volunteers help us find senior citizens in need.

2

Assess

We visit and listen to understand each senior citizen’s situation.

3

Deliver

Outreach day: food, health checks, essentials and fellowship.

4

Follow up

Return visits and calls, so no one is forgotten again.
