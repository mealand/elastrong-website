Who we are

Across Abuja, many elders live without family, income or care. We find them, we show up, and we keep coming back with food, medicine, clothing and the dignity of being remembered.

What we do

All programmes
01

Food & Nutrition

Food packs and hot meals delivered straight to elders who can no longer provide for themselves.

 02

Health & Wellness

Free check-ups, blood sugar and malaria tests, dental care and medication.

 03

Clothing & Essentials

Clothes, toiletries, mattresses and mosquito nets for a safer, more comfortable life.

 04

Companionship

Visits, conversation, prayer and laughter, because loneliness hurts too.
