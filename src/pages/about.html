Love, with its sleeves rolled up.

We are a Nigerian non-profit caring for elderly people who have no one else to care for them.

Our story

It began with one visit.

One afternoon, an elderly woman living alone, hungry and unwell, opened our eyes to a quiet crisis in our own neighbourhoods.

Many elders in Abuja’s suburbs and villages have outlived their families, lost their income, or been left behind as children move away. They are too often invisible.

“If we can’t change the whole world for them, we can change their world, one visit at a time.”

Elastrong Cares Foundation was born to change that. Today, our volunteers travel into communities with food, medicine, clothing and time, treating every elder like family.

Mission

Restore dignity & comfort

To meet the basic needs of vulnerable, neglected elderly people through compassionate, consistent outreach.

Vision

No elder left alone

A Nigeria where every elder ages with food on the table, care for their health, and people who love them.

What guides us

Our values

Compassion

Presence

Integrity
