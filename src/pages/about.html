Love, with its sleeves rolled up.

We are a Nigerian non-profit caring for elderly people who have no one else to care for them.

Our story

It began with one visit.

One afternoon, an elderly woman living alone, hungry and unwell, opened our eyes to a quiet crisis in our own neighbourhoods.

Many senior citizens in Nigeria’s suburbs and villages have outlived their families, lost their income, or been left behind as children move away. They are too often invisible.

“If we can’t change the whole world for them, we can change their world, one visit at a time.”

Elastrong Cares Foundation was born to change that. Today, our volunteers travel into communities with food, medicine, clothing and time, treating every senior citizen like family.

Mission

Our Mission

To provide quality healthcare, educational support, proper hygiene and healthy living in society to improve lives.

Vision

Our Vision

  • To show kindness, humanity and love to all, and promote proper hygiene and healthy living in society.
  • To have an honest, drug-free and God-fearing youth in society.
  • To pay attention to the older generation, widows and orphans in society.
