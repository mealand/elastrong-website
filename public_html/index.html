Who we are

Across Nigeria, many senior citizens live without family, income or care. We find them, we show up, and we keep coming back with food, medicine, clothing and the dignity of being remembered.

All programmes
01

Food & Nutrition

Food packs and hot meals delivered straight to senior citizens who can no longer provide for themselves.

 02

Medical Outreach

Free malaria tests & treatment, dental check-ups, consultations, blood pressure and sugar checks.

 03

Clothing & Essentials

Clothes, toiletries, mattresses and mosquito nets for a safer, more comfortable life.

 04

Companionship

Visits, conversation, prayer and laughter, because loneliness hurts too.
