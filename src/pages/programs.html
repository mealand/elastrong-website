Time & presence
04

Companionship

Loneliness wounds as deeply as hunger. We listen, laugh, pray and celebrate with the elders we serve.

  • Home visits & check-in calls
  • Birthday & festive celebrations
  • Sponsor-an-elder relationships
Project Clean
05

Hygiene & healthy living

Through Project Clean, volunteers roll up their sleeves to sweep, scrub and wash, leaving elders’ homes and surroundings clean, safe and healthy.

  • Cleaning homes & compounds
  • Washing clothes & bedding
  • Hygiene kits & healthy-living tips

How an outreach works

From discovery to follow-up.

1

Identify

Community leaders and volunteers help us find elders in need.

2

Assess

We visit and listen to understand each elder’s situation.

3

Deliver

Outreach day: food, health checks, essentials and fellowship.

4

Follow up

Return visits and calls, so no one is forgotten again.

Where we work

From city edges to rural villages.

We focus on underserved communities across the nation.
